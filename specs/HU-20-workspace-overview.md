# HU-20: Redesign the workspace overview

- **Status:** Implemented _(2026-10-15)_
- **Epic:** EP-01 · **Priority:** P1 · **Dependencies:** HU-17, HU-18, HU-19

## Goal

Prioritize monthly equivalent, annual projection, upcoming charges and savings in an API-backed
workspace overview.

## Acceptance criteria

- The overview displays existing monthly and annual values from the API.
- Multiple currencies remain separated.
- Upcoming charges are visible without opening another screen.
- Potential savings are represented when available.
- Loading, empty, success and error states remain functional.
- No business data is hardcoded as the final source.
- Overview actions open detailed feature screens.
